About

I've spent nearly three years in-house at D4 Interactive, working on organic growth for ContentStudio, a social media management platform used by marketing teams and agencies. My title is Digital Marketing Executive. In practice, most of my work is SEO.

My strongest area is off-page SEO. I find and vet publishers, build working relationships with them, and earn placements that point authority at the pages that matter commercially: comparison pages, feature pages and the parent pages of our content clusters. On the site itself, I rework content around search intent and organise it so that readers and search engines can follow it.

More answers now come from AI systems. I treat that as an extension of SEO rather than a replacement: clear answers on the page, consistent information about the brand, and credible third-party sites that mention it.

What I work on

Off-page SEO

Link acquisition through guest posts, listicle inclusions, link insertions and broken-link opportunities. Publisher partnerships, competitor backlink analysis and negotiation.

On-page SEO

Keyword research, search-intent mapping and content optimisation for blog, feature and comparison pages. Metadata, headings, FAQs and internal links.

Content strategy

Content clusters with clear parent and child pages, breadcrumbs, topical authority and competitor gap analysis.

AEO / GEO and AI search

Answer-first structure, FAQ and Article schema, entity context, and monitoring how the brand appears in AI-generated answers.

Online reputation management

Review-platform and directory listings, consistent business details and customer review campaigns.

Technical SEO fundamentals

Indexation and crawl errors in Google Search Console, XML sitemaps, redirects, canonical tags, broken links and basic Screaming Frog audits.
