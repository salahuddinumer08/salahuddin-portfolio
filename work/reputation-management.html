Strengthening reputation on review platforms

Making sure that buyers comparing tools find ContentStudio listed accurately, with recent reviews from real customers.

My role
Online reputation management
Platforms
G2, Capterra, Trustpilot and directories
Focus
Listings, consistency and reviews

Context

SaaS buyers check review platforms before shortlisting a tool, and the same platforms often appear in search results and AI answers about a category. Missing, unclaimed or inconsistent listings weaken that first impression.

What I did

  • Audited listings across review platforms and business directories, then claimed unverified profiles.
  • Standardised business details (name, address and contact information) so every listing matched.
  • Added new listings on platforms and directories relevant to the category, and skipped ones that weren't.
  • Ran review campaigns with existing customers, encouraging them to share their experience on G2, Capterra, Trustpilot and other platforms.

My part

I identified the platforms, handled the listings and coordinated the customer review requests.

Outcome

A more complete and consistent presence for ContentStudio across third-party review platforms, which supports customer trust and gives search engines and AI systems consistent information about the brand.

Work for ContentStudio, a product of D4 Interactive. Figures come from internal reporting and SEO tools. Dashboards are confidential, but I'm happy to explain how each number was measured.
