Building authority for commercial and comparison pages

A link acquisition programme for ContentStudio, aimed at the pages that turn searches into sign-ups.

My role
Led off-page SEO
Period
Oct 2024 – Jul 2026 (logged placements)
Focus
Link acquisition and publisher partnerships

Context

ContentStudio competes in social media management, where the commercial keywords, such as “social media management tools” and “social media scheduler”, are contested by large, well-known brands. Pages that rank for those terms need strong, relevant authority behind them.

The challenge

Relevant marketing and SaaS publishers receive a steady stream of link requests and ignore generic pitches. The job was to earn placements at a steady rate, on sites that are relevant to the product, and to point them at the pages that matter commercially rather than wherever a link happened to be available.

What I did

  • Found opportunities. Built prospect lists from competitor backlink analysis in Ahrefs, from roundups ranking for target keywords and from broken-link research: relevant sites linking to dead resources, where a ContentStudio page could replace them.
  • Vetted every site for topical relevance, organic traffic, authority and whether its pages were indexed, before any outreach.
  • Researched contacts and pitched specifically: listicle inclusions, guest posts, contextual link insertions and broken-link replacements, each matched to what the publisher's page needed.
  • Chose target pages deliberately. Links went to comparison pages, feature pages and the parent pages of content clusters, with anchors mapped to the keywords each page targets.
  • Kept relationships going. Many placements came from repeat work with publishers, SaaS brands, marketing agencies and SEO partners, including co-marketing partnerships where we featured each other's products in relevant roundups. I also negotiated paid collaborations with creators, including product walkthrough videos.
  • Tracked everything in a placement log with status and indexing, and rechecked links to confirm they stayed live.

My part

I ran the process end to end: prospecting, vetting, contact research, outreach, negotiation, placement and follow-up.

Outcome

  • 400+ backlinks from 350+ referring domains between October 2024 and July 2026, with a median Domain Rating of around 70.
  • An Ahrefs check in October 2026 found 252 of the 381 domains in my log still linking to ContentStudio, with a median Domain Rating of 72. 165 of them are DR 70 or above.
  • Referring domains to the main “social media management tools” page grew from 41 to 210 between September 2024 and October 2026.
  • Moz Domain Authority rose from 37 to 48 within a year while I led off-page SEO.
  • Co-marketing listicle partnerships with established SaaS brands, including Zapier, Make.com and HubSpot, in which each side featured the other's product in relevant roundups.

Domain Authority and Domain Rating are third-party estimates, not Google metrics. I use them as a rough guide to site strength, alongside relevance and real traffic.
