Getting a SaaS brand into AI-generated answers

Making ContentStudio easier for AI answer engines to understand, and more present on the pages they draw from.

My role
AEO / GEO and AI search visibility
Channels
Google AI Overviews and AI assistants
Focus
Content structure and third-party mentions

Context

More people now ask an AI assistant or read an AI Overview before visiting a site, often with a question like “what's the best social media management tool?”. These answers draw on pages that already rank, on roundups and listicles, and on what credible third-party sites say about a brand.

What I did

  • Made content easy to extract. I restructured articles with question-led headings, direct answers near the top, FAQ sections, consistent entity details about the product, and FAQ and Article schema.
  • Chose outreach targets by where answers come from. For money keywords such as scheduler terms, I checked which listicles rank in Google and which are cited in AI-generated answers, researching more than 90 of them, then pursued inclusion on those pages.
  • Strengthened third-party signals through brand mentions on relevant sites and consistent profiles on review platforms.
  • Monitored brand presence by checking how ContentStudio appears in AI-generated answers for priority questions.

My part

I worked on content structure and markup, the listicle research and outreach, and the monitoring.

How I think about results

AI answers vary from one run to the next, and Google says there are no special requirements for appearing in AI Overviews beyond normal SEO. So I don't attribute a single citation to a single change. What the data does show is why the work matters: in October 2026, visitors arriving from AI assistants signed up at about five times ContentStudio's site-wide rate. What I can point to is the work itself: content that is easier to extract, inclusion on roundups that AI answers draw from, and a more consistent third-party footprint for the brand.

In data
