Relaunching an affiliate programme

Taking over ContentStudio's affiliate programme and relaunching it to attract and equip more partners.

My role
Affiliate programme lead
Period
From September 2026
Platform
FirstPromoter

Context

An affiliate programme only grows if promoters can see why it's worth their time and have what they need to promote the product well. When I took over in September 2026, the programme pages and promotional assets needed updating.

What I did

  • Rebuilt the offer. I updated the programme pages and terms and moved to tiered commissions that rise with referrals.
  • Set up new FirstPromoter campaigns with tier segments.
  • Refreshed the asset library with demo and walkthrough videos, feature and comparison pages, banners and email templates.
  • Recruited deliberately. I re-engaged existing affiliates, contacted creators and publishers who promote comparable SaaS tools, and pitched affiliate-programme roundup sites to feature the programme.

My part

I ran the relaunch: offer, campaign setup, assets and recruitment outreach.

Outcome

In the first two months (September to October 2026):

  • About 150 new affiliates joined.
  • Affiliate clicks rose 56% compared with the previous period.
  • About 100 sign-ups came through affiliate referrals, some of whom became paying customers.

Work for ContentStudio, a product of D4 Interactive. Figures come from internal reporting and SEO tools. Dashboards are confidential, but I'm happy to explain how each number was measured.
